# ZOLV™ — Logo Redesign, Premium Packaging & DTC Website Redesign

> **Brand Identity, Industrial Packaging System & DTC E-Commerce Redesign**  
> Direct answer to the client's design contest brief for **ZOLV Oral Bio-Films** (Competitor to *The 5TRIP / the5trip.com*, *Daps*, and *Sleep or Die*).

---

## 🎯 How This Solution Solves the Client's 3 Core Requirements

### 1. Logo Redesign
- **The Problem:** Generic logos or AI-generated blobs lack intention and readability. The client explicitly noted: *"pls stop uploading just chatgpt images without efforts .. we can do that ourself.. looking for decent designer... Any dissolving or dripping detail should be subtle and intentional."*
- **Our Solution:** 
  - A bespoke, razor-sharp geometric wordmark with 45-degree forward-thrust terminal cuts on the **Z** (representing athletic speed and instant sublingual absorption).
  - An intentional **4-stage nanometer particulate dissolve motif** extending from the apex of the letter **V**. This symbolizes the sublingual bio-film dissolving upon salivary contact without muddling the letterforms.
  - 100% scalable vector geometry that holds clarity whether rendered on a 30mm foil pouch or a billboard.

### 2. Pocket-Size Flip-Top Packaging System
- **The Problem:** Bulky cans, rattling pill bottles, and powder tubs are inconvenient for everyday carry.
- **Our Solution:** 
  - Compact **Flip-Top Pocket Dispenser (75mm × 45mm × 12mm)** — smaller than an AirPods case, flatter than a phone.
  - Houses **30 individually sealed oral strip sachets** (40mm × 30mm multi-ply nitrogen-flushed barrier foil with easy-tear notches).
  - Rigid 350 GSM Solid Bleached Sulfate (SBS) cardstock with a soft-touch velvet matte black finish, raised spot-UV gloss wordmark, and metallic foil stamping for SKU color codes.

### 3. Product Family Across 4 SKUs
- **ENERGY (SKU 01):** Solar Citrus Mint (`#FF5500` / Pantone 021 C + Hot Foil Amber) — 50mg Caffeine + 50mg L-Theanine + B12.
- **SLEEP (SKU 02):** Midnight Blackberry Lavender (`#8B5CF6` / Pantone 2665 C + Lucid Violet) — 3mg Melatonin + GABA + Tart Cherry.
- **FOCUS (SKU 03):** Cyber Alpine Matcha (`#00F59B` / Pantone 802 C Neon + Emerald Foil) — 150mg Lion's Mane + 100mg Alpha-GPC + Bacopa.
- **NAD+ (SKU 04):** Glacial Arctic Yuzu (`#00D4FF` / Pantone 801 C + Holographic Silver Foil) — 150mg Liposomal NMN + Resveratrol.
