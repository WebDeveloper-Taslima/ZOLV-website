DELIVERABLE 01

THE REFINED ZOLV WORDMARK

The Brief Requirement: "Improve the existing ZOLV wordmark while retaining its recognizable personality. Explore a more refined, balanced and premium execution. Any dissolving or dripping detail should be subtle and intentional."

1. Kinetic Terminal Cuts

The letter Z features a forward-thrust 45-degree speed chamfer, evoking speed of onset (instant sublingual absorption) and athletic energy akin to Red Bull and Nike.

2. Geometric Precision & Optical Kerning

The O has an engineered circular silhouette with balanced stroke modulation, ensuring perfect clarity at both billboard scale and on a 30mm foil sachet.

3. Subtle & Intentional Sublingual Dissolve

Rather than messy melting drips that compromise readability, the dissolve effect is executed as an intentional 4-stage micro-particle dispersion off the apex of the letter V. This directly symbolizes the oral film dissolving into nanometer bioactives on the tongue.
